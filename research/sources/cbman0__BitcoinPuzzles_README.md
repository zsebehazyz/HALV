## Era Notes: 2012–2017 (Old/Forgotten Era)

The earliest Bitcoin puzzles (2011–2017) are the most likely to be:
- Forgotten and abandoned with funds still locked
- Available on Wayback Machine / archive.org only
- Posted casually on Bitcointalk with no formal archiving

**Best research strategies for old puzzles:**
1. Search Bitcointalk.org for threads tagged "puzzle" or "riddle" from 2012–2017
2. Use Wayback Machine on bitcointalk.org puzzle threads
3. Google dork: `site:bitcointalk.org "bitcoin puzzle" "private key" 2013`
4. Google dork: `site:bitcointalk.org "hidden" "BTC" "solve" before:2017`
5. Check archive.org for defunct Bitcoin puzzle websites
6. The Casascius coins (2011–2013) era represents thousands of still-unredeemed physical tokens
