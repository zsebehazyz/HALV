### Level 5 (Relaunched 2021) — UNSOLVED
**Prize:** 0.005 BTC
**Solve Time:** 3+ years (original flawed version); relaunch 2021 — still UNSOLVED
**Status:** **UNSOLVED**
**URLs:** https://privatekeys.pw/puzzles/0.005-btc-lvl5-puzzle | https://github.com/HomelessPhD/Zden_LVL5

**Full Description:**
Level 5 was first released in 2018 and went unsolved for 3+ years. Zden eventually discovered the original puzzle had a flaw (the encoding was incomplete or broken), and relaunched it in 2021 with corrections.

**Encoding Method (Relaunched Version):**
> "Sum of two rectangles' areas = one byte of the private key"

The image contains colored rectangles of varying sizes. For each consecutive pair of rectangles:
- Calculate the **pixel area** of each (width × height)
- **Sum the two areas**
- The sum = one **byte value** (0–255) of the private key
- All bytes assembled in order = the complete private key

HomelessPhD maintains a community solving repo: https://github.com/HomelessPhD/Zden_LVL5

### Level XM17 (Animated GIF)
**Prize:** Undisclosed
**Solve Time:** **22 hours**
**Status:** SOLVED

**Encoding Method:**
The puzzle used an animated GIF format. The private key bytes were encoded across specific frames — each frame contained pixel data that, at the correct position, yielded one byte of the key. Solvers had to analyze individual frames rather than the animation as a whole.

## Notes on Zden's Design Philosophy

Zden's puzzles all share the same deceptively simple premise — "find the private key in this image" — but the encoding varies completely between puzzles. This means:

1. **No meta-knowledge** carries over between puzzles — each one must be approached fresh
2. The community can't build a "solver" for "Zden puzzles" — each requires unique reverse-engineering
3. The image IS the puzzle — no external hints, no websites, no chat bots
4. Solve times ranging from 6 hours to "never" suggest excellent calibration across difficulty levels

The demoscene background is crucial: demosceners are trained to find creative solutions under constraints, extract maximum information from minimum data, and appreciate technical elegance. Zden's puzzles reward exactly these skills.
